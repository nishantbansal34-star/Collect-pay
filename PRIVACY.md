# Orbe Pay — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Orbe Pay is an app made by Nishant Bansal (Aligarh, India) that shows a UPI payment QR code with the amount already filled in. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version
**Everything stays on your phone.** Orbe Pay has no internet permission, so it cannot send your UPI IDs, amounts or any other data anywhere. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

## Orbe Pay does not handle money
Orbe Pay only creates a standard UPI QR code. The customer pays with their own UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or any other). Orbe Pay never sees, processes, stores or confirms payments, bank details, card numbers or UPI PINs. Always confirm incoming payments in your own bank or UPI app.

## What the app stores on your phone
Only in the app's private storage on your device:
- **The QR accounts you add:** a name for each one, your UPI ID, the name customers see, and its colour.
- **Settings**, such as which QR was used last.

Amounts and notes you type are used only to draw the QR code and are not saved.

## Sharing
When you tap **Share QR**, the app creates a payment card image (QR code, amount, your name and UPI ID) and opens Android's share menu. The image goes only to the app and person you choose.

## Permissions
Orbe Pay requests no special permissions.

## Deleting your data
Delete any saved QR from **Edit QR → Delete**, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

## Children
Orbe Pay is meant for shop owners and businesses. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
