## English
**App name (30):** Orbe Pay: UPI Payment QR
**Short description (80):** Type the amount, show the QR. Customers scan and pay with any UPI app.

**Full description:**
Orbe Pay makes collecting UPI payments at your shop fast and beautiful. Type the amount on the keypad, tap Request, and show your customer a QR code with the amount already filled in. They scan it with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or any UPI app and simply approve.

⚡ Built for the counter
• Big glass keypad: no phone keyboard popping up
• Quick amounts (₹100 to ₹5,000) and a note for the customer ("Order #214")
• Full-screen QR that keeps your screen awake
• New request in one tap for the next customer

💳 All your accounts
• Save several UPI IDs as metal cards: shop counter, wholesale, personal
• Swipe the QR card to switch accounts

📲 Share on WhatsApp
• Share QR sends a ready-made payment card image with the amount, your name and UPI ID

🔒 Private and safe
• Orbe Pay only creates the QR. Money goes straight from your customer's UPI app to your bank. Orbe Pay never touches payments, bank details or PINs.
• No internet permission, no ads, no accounts. Your UPI IDs stay on your phone.

Always confirm incoming payments in your own bank or UPI app.
